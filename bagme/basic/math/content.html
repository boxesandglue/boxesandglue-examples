Mathematics with bagme

Every formula below is presentation MathML embedded directly in the HTML handed to bagme. The MathML reader turns the <math> subtree into glyphs through the OpenType-MATH engine, and — because the document is PDF/UA-2 — each one is tagged as a Formula structure element.
